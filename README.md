# Dataset
I chose the [St. Louis Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED)](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/) from the Awesome Data Science list.

**Description:** The FRED database includes is a collection of economic datasets maintained by the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis. These various datasets include economic indicators such as real gdp, unemployment rate, cpi, gold, etc. FRED datasets have many potential applications for all industries. It is used widely in economic research to study relationships between economic indicators over time. Economists also study the relationship between outside variables and these indicators through regression analyses. FRED datasets can also be utilized by many businesses to track economic trends in order to assist their decisions.
